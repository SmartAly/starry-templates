# Prompt: mobile login & onboarding

Copy the block below into Starry's canvas input.

---

Build three phone screens (390 × 844) side by side, sharing one design system:

1. **Login** — centered wordmark, "Welcome back" heading, email and password
   fields with floating labels, a "Forgot password?" link, a full-width green
   "Sign in" button, a divider "or continue with", and two social buttons.
2. **Verification** — six OTP boxes, a resend timer, and a numeric keypad
   hint at the bottom.
3. **Onboarding** — full-bleed illustration area, page indicator with three dots,
   title "Describe it. Starry builds it.", one-line body copy, and a bottom
   primary button "Get started".

Use the Base UI design system: dark theme (#15151A background), green (#00A737)
primary, cyan→violet gradient for AI accents, 12px card radius, auto-layout,
safe-area aware, 16px minimum tap targets.
